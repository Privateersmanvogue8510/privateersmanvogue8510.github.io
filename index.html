🔬 Technical Highlights

For the curious minds, MeshCompute is built on modern technology:

  • Peer-to-Peer Network: No middleman servers. Your computer talks directly with others.
  • NAT Hole-Punching: Works even behind home routers. Automatically finds a path through any firewall.
  • GGUF & llama-cpp support: Runs popular open-source AI models like Llama, Mistral, and more.
  • OpenAI API Compatible: Connect any existing AI app that uses the OpenAI standard – it just works.
  • Verifiable Ledger: Proof-of-computation using blockchain concepts. You can trust the network.

🛑 Troubleshooting

Problem: Windows SmartScreen shows a warning

Solution: Click "More info" then "Run anyway". This is because the app is new and not yet flagged by Microsoft.


Problem: "MeshCompute won't start"

Solution: Make sure your Windows is up to date. Run the app as Administrator by right-clicking it and selecting "Run as administrator".


Problem: "Stuck at 'Connecting...'"

Solution: Your firewall may be blocking it. Click "Allow access" when prompted.
